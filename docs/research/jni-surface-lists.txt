### SECTION L -- guest TLS requirement (not JNI, but it blocks everything)
  1,282 `MRS Xt, TPIDR_EL0` instructions in .text.
  1,276 of them are immediately followed by `LDR Xn,[Xt,#0x28]` == bionic TLS slot 5
  (TLS_SLOT_STACK_GUARD). One site reads +0x4c0. Every stack-protected function in
  libroblox.so therefore reads the bionic thread-pointer layout directly; Omnidroid must
  program TPIDR_EL0 per guest thread with a bionic-shaped TLS block or every prologue
  mis-compares and every epilogue calls __stack_chk_fail.
